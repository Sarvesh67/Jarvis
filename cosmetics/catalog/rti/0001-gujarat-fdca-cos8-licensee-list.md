# RTI 0001 — Gujarat FDCA, COS-8 / COS-9 licensee list

**Status** drafted, not filed · **Target** Food and Drug Control Administration, Gujarat
**Why Gujarat first** it is the project's Udyam spine and largest cluster, and it reports
**zero** cosmetics licences on ONDLS (see `catalog/sources/s02_state_fda.yaml`). It is the
single highest-value jurisdiction and it is invisible to every national system tested.

## Filing notes

- The applicant must be an Indian citizen; the request must be filed by a person, not by
  this project. **Sarvesh files this — it cannot be automated.**
- Fee is ₹10. Gujarat runs its own RTI portal rather than the central `rtionline.gov.in`,
  which covers central public authorities only. Confirm the current state portal and the
  FDCA's designated PIO address before filing.
- Expect a reply within 30 days under s.7(1).
- The drafting below is deliberate on two points: it asks for information **in the
  electronic form already held**, to pre-empt a s.7(9) "disproportionate diversion of
  resources" refusal; and it asks only for firm-level fields, to pre-empt a s.8(1)(j)
  personal-information refusal.

## What to do with the answer

Firm name, licence number, **category endorsed** and validity are what matter. The category
field is the one this project cannot get anywhere else — it is the only route found so far
to a tier-1 `PERMITS` edge. If the reply carries it, re-read `decisions/0012` before
loading: 0012 says PERMITS stays empty *because no held source can evidence it*, and a
state SLA's own register would be exactly such a source. That is a decision to revisit
deliberately, not to quietly overwrite.

**DPDP.** A proprietorship licence may be held in an individual's name. Where the licensee
is a proprietorship, the row gets `person`-table handling — consent basis recorded,
deletable — not industrial-spine handling. This is the hard rule no test catches.

## Draft

To,
The Public Information Officer,
Food and Drug Control Administration (FDCA),
Government of Gujarat, Gandhinagar.

**Subject:** Request for information under the Right to Information Act, 2005

Sir / Madam,

Under Section 6(1) of the Right to Information Act, 2005, I request the following
information concerning licences to manufacture cosmetics issued by the State Licensing
Authority, Gujarat, under the Cosmetics Rules, 2020:

1. A list of all **currently valid licences in Form COS-8** (licence to manufacture
   cosmetics for sale or distribution) issued by the State Licensing Authority, Gujarat,
   showing for each: licence number, date of issue, date of expiry, name of the licensee
   firm, address of the approved manufacturing premises, and the **categories of cosmetics
   endorsed on the licence**.

2. The same list for all currently valid licences in **Form COS-9** (loan licence),
   additionally showing the name and address of the licensee on whose premises manufacture
   is permitted.

3. The **total number** of valid COS-8 and COS-9 licences on the register as on the date
   of this application, stated separately.

4. Whether the Authority maintains the above register in electronic form, and if so in
   what format.

I request that the information at (1) and (2) be supplied **in the electronic form in
which it is already maintained** — a spreadsheet or database export, by email or on
electronic media — rather than compiled afresh. I am not seeking any record that would
require the Authority to create or collate new information within the meaning of the Act.

I am **not** seeking any personal information of any individual, any application under
process, any inspection report, or any commercial confidence of any licensee. My request is
limited to the fact and scope of licences granted, which is regulatory information held in
the public interest.

If any part of this request is held to be exempt, I request that the remainder be provided
under Section 10(1), together with the reasons for severance.

Application fee of ₹10 is enclosed / paid as per the prescribed mode.
